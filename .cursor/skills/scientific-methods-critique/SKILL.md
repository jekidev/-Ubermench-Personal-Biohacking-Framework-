# Scientific methods critique

When reviewing a paper or intervention claim:

1. Separate mechanistic plausibility from human outcomes.
2. Name the design (RCT, cohort, N-of-1, in vitro).
3. Flag small n, no control, surrogate endpoints, and conflicts.
4. Do not promote the claim into the intervention ranker without an evidence grade.
