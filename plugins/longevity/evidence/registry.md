# Longevity evidence registry v0.1

Every intervention entry should record:

- evidence grade
- population
- endpoint type
- study design
- sample size
- effect estimate when available
- follow-up duration
- known harms
- date last reviewed
- source identifiers

## Evidence hierarchy

**A — Human randomized evidence for hard clinical outcomes**

Mortality, major cardiovascular events, cancer outcomes or other clinically meaningful outcomes supported by high-quality randomized evidence.

**B — Human randomized evidence for validated intermediate outcomes**

Strong intervention evidence, but without demonstrated lifespan extension.

**C — Human observational / mechanistic evidence**

Useful for hypothesis generation but vulnerable to confounding or surrogate-endpoint limitations.

**D — Preclinical evidence**

Animal or cellular evidence without adequate human efficacy evidence.

**E — Speculative**

Hypothesis, weak evidence or claims not yet supported by reliable data.

## Required distinction

The UI must never translate a low-grade longevity hypothesis into a claim of increased human lifespan. "Potential aging mechanism" and "proven mortality benefit" are separate fields.
