# Longevity intervention registry v0.1

The registry is intentionally evidence-tiered.

## Tier 1 — established prevention / treatment when indicated

- Blood-pressure management according to clinical indication and tolerance
- LDL/ApoB lowering according to cardiovascular risk
- Physical activity and cardiorespiratory fitness
- Resistance training and preservation of lean mass
- Smoking/substance avoidance
- Vaccination and evidence-based screening
- Sleep optimization

## Tier 2 — promising geroscience

- mTOR modulation / rapamycin and rapalogs
- Senolytic approaches
- Metabolic interventions including metformin
- Glycation/AGE-targeting strategies
- Emerging immune and regenerative approaches

## Tier 3 — research watchlist

- Partial epigenetic reprogramming
- Cellular rejuvenation and regeneration
- Advanced gene editing
- Tissue engineering and organ replacement
- Neuroregeneration

## Current user-tracked intervention examples

The plugin can store medications such as nebivolol, tadalafil or metformin as user-entered interventions. The registry records indication, target metric, exposure, evidence status and safety observations; it does not infer that a drug is a longevity treatment merely because it is being tracked.
