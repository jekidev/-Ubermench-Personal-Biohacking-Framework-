## Overview cards

- Blood pressure 7-day average
- Resting heart rate
- HRV trend when available
- Weight/body composition trend
- ApoB/LDL-C trend
- HbA1c/glucose trend
- eGFR/ACR trend
- CRP trend
- Fitness trend
- Active safety flags

Cards must link to the underlying observations rather than displaying derived values without provenance.
