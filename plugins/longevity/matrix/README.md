# Longevity Health Matrix

The matrix groups confirmed observations into transparent health domains. It is a coverage and trend-navigation layer, not a medical score or diagnosis.

## Domains

- Cardiovascular
- Metabolic
- Inflammation
- Renal
- Hepatic
- Hematology
- Fitness
- Recovery

## Rules

- No single biomarker becomes a disease score.
- Missing data is shown as `none`, not as a poor health result.
- One biomarker is `limited` coverage; two or more distinct mapped biomarkers are `good` coverage.
- Original values, units, dates and provenance remain available.
- Future risk models must be separate, evidence-backed modules and must not overwrite this observational layer.
